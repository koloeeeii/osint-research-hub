# Case Template

Copy this structure into a new case folder before starting an investigation.

**Case ID:**  
**Title:**  
**Case type:** Fictional simulation / Public-record research  
**Status:** Planned / In progress / Completed  
**Date started:**  
**Last updated:**  

## 1. Intelligence question

State one precise, answerable question.

## 2. Scope and collection plan

What sources and time frame are in scope? What is intentionally excluded? Are there legal, privacy, or ethical limits?

## 3. Source register

Reference source IDs in the evidence log. Record URLs, dates, source types, and limitations.

## 4. Findings

For each finding, cite the supporting source ID. Clearly label any inference.

## 5. Competing hypotheses

List plausible alternative explanations and what evidence supports or contradicts each.

## 6. Intelligence gaps

Identify consequential missing information. Describe lawful and proportionate next research steps.

## 7. Analytical assessment

Summarize the judgment and its implications. State confidence (low / moderate / high) with a reason.

## 8. Limitations and corrections

Explain source limitations, uncertainties, and any material changes to earlier assessments.

## 9. References

Provide direct links to public primary sources where possible.

*Do not invent evidence or imply that simulated case data are authentic. Protect personal privacy and follow applicable laws and platform terms.*
