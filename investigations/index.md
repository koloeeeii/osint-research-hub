## Standard for publication

Each completed case should contain a research question, scope, source register, findings tied to evidence, plausible alternative explanations, intelligence gaps, analytical confidence, and limitations. See the [case template]({{ '/templates/case-template.md' | relative_url }}).

**Training exercises are not real investigations.** Public-record case studies will use only legitimately available information and will not identify or accuse private individuals without an appropriate, documented public-interest basis.
